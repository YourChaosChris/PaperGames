Two-player strategy games

  • Gomoku (Five in a Row) – Get five stones in a row first, on a Go-sized board.
  • Senet – One of the oldest known board games, from ancient Egypt.
  • Shogi – Japanese chess, where captured pieces switch sides and rejoin the battle.
  • Onitama – A fast, elegant duel decided by five shifting move cards.
  • Hnefatafl (Viking Chess) – An asymmetric Norse game: the king must escape, the attackers must trap him.
  • Quoridor – Race to the far side of the board while building walls to block your opponent.
  • Hex – Connect your two sides of a hexagonal board before your opponent connects theirs.
  • Halma – Race all your pieces across the board into your opponent's starting camp.

Single-player puzzles

  • Sudoku – Fill a 9x9 grid with digits so every row, column and 3x3 box contains 1-9 exactly once.
  • Peg Solitaire – Jump pegs over each other on a cross-shaped board until only one remains.
  • Minesweeper – Uncover every safe square using the number clues, without triggering a hidden mine.
  • Nonograms (Picross) – Use row and column number clues to reveal a hidden picture, one cell at a time.
  • 2048 – Slide and merge numbered tiles to reach 2048 before the board fills up.
  • Mahjong Solitaire – Clear the layered tile layout by matching identical pairs.
  • FreeCell – The classic card solitaire where nearly every deal can be won with the right moves.
