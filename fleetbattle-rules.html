Fleet Battle Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays Fleet Battle - useful if you're rusty, or learning.

The basic idea

The fleet
Each side has five ships: a Carrier (5 cells), a Battleship (4 cells), a Cruiser (3 cells), a Submarine (3 cells), and a Destroyer (2 cells). Every ship occupies a straight horizontal or vertical run of consecutive cells on a 10x10 grid.
Placement
Before battle begins, you place your five ships on your own grid; the computer places its fleet automatically and keeps it hidden. This app uses the strict standard rule that ships may not touch each other, not even diagonally - some casual variants allow touching, but that variant is not offered here.

Taking your turn

Firing
Players alternate turns, one shot per turn regardless of whether it hits or misses (no bonus shot for a hit). Click a cell on the enemy grid to fire; the result - hit or miss - is revealed immediately.
Sinking a ship
When every cell of a ship has been hit, it is sunk, and its full outline is revealed on the board.

How a game ends

Winning
The first side to sink all five of the opponent's ships wins.
Resignation
You can resign at any time during battle, ending the game immediately in the computer's favor.
