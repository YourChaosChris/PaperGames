Mau Mau Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays Mau Mau - useful if you're rusty, or learning. House rules vary a lot; these are the ones used here.

The basic idea

The cards
An ordinary 32-card pack: Seven, Eight, Nine, Ten, Jack, Queen, King and Ace in each of the four suits - spades, hearts, diamonds and clubs.
Dealing
Each of the 2-4 players gets 5 cards. One card is turned face up to start the discard pile - it has no special effect - and the rest are the stock.
Playing a card
On your turn, play one card that matches the top card of the discard pile in suit or in rank. A Jack fits on anything.
Drawing
If you can't play, draw one card from the stock. If it fits, you may play it straight away or keep it; otherwise your turn ends.

Special cards

Seven
The next player has to draw two cards. If they can play a Seven themselves, the penalty passes on to the player after them and grows by two for every Seven. Whoever takes the cards draws them all at once, and that ends their turn.
Eight
The next player misses their turn.
Jack
Whoever plays a Jack names a suit by tapping one of four big buttons. The next card has to be of that suit - or another Jack.
Ace
Whoever plays an Ace goes again straight away.

Winning the game

An empty stock
When the stock runs out, the discard pile except its top card is shuffled and becomes the new stock.
Going out
The first player to play their last card wins.
Calling "Mau"
At the table, a player down to their last card has to call "Mau" or take a penalty. Here the game calls it for you and shows it, and there is no penalty - on a slow e-ink screen it would only cause frustration, not fun.

Playing on the screen

Piles and hand
Tap the stock to draw. Tap a card in your hand to play it; cards that can't be played right now sit a little lower with a dashed border. The suit asked for with a Jack is shown next to the discard pile for as long as it applies. With several people on one device, each hand is covered between turns.
