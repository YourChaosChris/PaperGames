Klappenkasten Rules

Close numbered flaps by matching the dice.

A turn

Start with flaps 1 to 9 open. Roll two dice and close one or more open flaps whose numbers add up to the total. If flaps 7, 8 and 9 are all closed, roll one die instead. Roll again after every legal closure.

Scoring and winning

When no legal closure remains, the sum of open flaps is your score for the round. Each player takes two turns. The lowest total wins. Close every flap to shut the box and score zero for that turn.

The computer chooses a legal set of flaps with the largest total, a simple strategy that tends to remove high numbers early.
