Card Tactics Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays this card-driven duel - useful if you're rusty, or learning.

The basic idea

The setup
Each side starts with four pawns and one master lined up on their home row, the master in the center square. Five move cards are dealt at random from a shared deck: two go to each player, and one sits face-up in the middle as the spare.
Move cards
Every card shows a small set of relative moves any of your own pieces may make from wherever it stands - the same card works for a pawn or the master. There's no piece that ignores the cards: what a card allows is the only way to move that turn.
Making a move
On your turn, pick one of your two cards, then one of your own pieces, then a square that card allows for it - capturing any enemy piece standing there. The card you used then goes to the middle, and the card that was waiting there joins your hand in its place, so the set of five cards keeps rotating between both players all game.

Winning the game

Two ways to win
Capture the opponent's master, or march your own master onto their home shrine - the center square of their back row. Either one ends the game immediately.
