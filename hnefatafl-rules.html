Hnefatafl Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays this ancient Norse siege game (the Brandub variant, on a 7x7 board) - useful if you're rusty, or learning.

The basic idea

The setup
The king starts on the center throne, guarded by four defenders, besieged by eight attackers coming from the middle of each edge. Attackers move first, and the two sides have very different goals: the king's side wants him to escape, the attackers want to trap him.
Moving
Every piece - attacker, defender, or the king - moves like a rook: any distance in a straight line, but never through or onto another piece. The throne and the four corners are off-limits to everyone but the king, who may freely enter and leave them.
Capturing
A regular piece is captured by custodian capture: trap it between two enemy pieces (or one enemy piece and the throne or a corner) on opposite sides, and it's removed. Only the piece that just moved can trigger a capture, so moving a piece between two enemies yourself is always safe.

Winning the game

How the game ends
The king's side wins the moment he reaches any of the four corners. The attackers win by surrounding the king on all four sides with attackers (the edge of the board counts as one of those sides, so a king pinned against the edge can be trapped with only three).
