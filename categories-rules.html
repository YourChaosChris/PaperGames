Categories Rules

A quick reference for the classic pen-and-paper word game - and for what this page does as your game master.

The basic idea

Getting ready
Every player needs paper and a pen. Choose the categories together - for example City, Country, River, First name, Animal, Job - and write them as columns across the top of your sheet. Enter the players and the categories on this page, and choose a time limit if you like.
A round
Tap "Draw a letter". Everyone now tries to write one word per category that starts with this letter - a city beginning with B, a country beginning with B, and so on. Each letter is drawn only once per game.
Stopping
The round ends when the time runs out, or as soon as the first player has filled in every category and calls "Stop!" - tap the Stop button then. Everyone puts their pen down immediately.

Scoring

Points per answer
Read the answers out category by category. A unique answer scores 10 points, an answer someone else also wrote scores 5, and if only one player found anything at all for a category, that answer scores 20. A missing or invalid answer scores 0. Tap a cell in the table to cycle through 0, 5, 10 and 20, then tap "Add up round".
Doubtful answers
Whether an answer counts is decided by the group - if in doubt, by a majority vote. Agree before the game whether spelling mistakes or very obscure answers are allowed.
End of the game
Play as many rounds as you like, then tap "End game". Whoever has the most points wins.
