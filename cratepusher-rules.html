Crate Pusher Rules

How Crate Pusher works on PaperGames: a puzzle for one, about moving crates in a small storeroom.

The basic idea

The board
A rectangular grid of walls (solid black) and floor (white). On the floor stand you (a filled circle), several crates (a square with a cross) and exactly as many targets (a small hollow square). A crate standing on a target turns into a solid square.
Moving
You move one cell at a time, up, down, left or right - never diagonally. You can't walk through walls or crates.
Pushing
Walking into a crate pushes it one cell further, but only if the cell behind it is free: no wall and no second crate. You can only ever push one crate at a time, and you can never pull a crate back towards you.
The goal
The level is solved as soon as every crate stands on a target. It doesn't matter which crate goes on which target.
Getting stuck
Because crates can't be pulled, a careless push can make a level unsolvable - for example a crate pushed into a corner that is not a target. The game warns you when a crate is stuck in such a corner. Other dead ends are not announced: think ahead, and use "Undo move" as often as you like to go back.
Controls
Tap a cell next to you to step there (and push a crate standing there). Tap a floor cell further away and you walk there by the shortest way - but only if you can get there without pushing any crate. The arrow keys and W, A, S, D also move you. "Undo move" takes back your moves one by one, as many as you want; "Restart level" starts the level over.
The levels
Every level is made by PaperGames itself, fresh each time: easy on a 7x7 grid with 3 crates, medium on 9x9 with 4, hard on 10x10 with 5. The program builds each level backwards from the solved position, so it is always solvable, and then checks it once more with its own solver. The number of pushes the best solution needs is shown above the board, together with your steps, your pushes and how many crates already stand on a target.
