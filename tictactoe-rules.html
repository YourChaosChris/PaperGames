Tic-Tac-Toe Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays Tic-Tac-Toe, also known as Noughts and Crosses.

The basic idea

The board and marks
The board is a 3x3 grid of nine cells. Players take turns putting their mark in an empty cell: a cross for Player 1, a circle for Player 2. Player 1 always moves first. A mark stays where it is for the rest of the game.

How a game ends

Winning
The first player to get three of their own marks in a row - across, down or diagonally - wins at once. The winning line is shown in solid black.
Draw
If all nine cells are filled and nobody has three in a row, the game is a draw. Two players who make no mistakes always draw - so on the Hard level the computer never loses, and the best you can get against it is a draw.
Resignation
A player can resign at any time, ending the game immediately in the opponent's favor.

The Hard level

On Hard the computer works through every possible continuation and never makes a mistake. For the 3x3 board it has been proven by checking every possible game that two flawless players always draw, so a draw is the best you can get against it - one mistake and you lose.
