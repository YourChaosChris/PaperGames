Reversi Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays Reversi (also historically known as Othello) - useful if you're rusty, or learning.

The basic idea

Discs and the start
Reversi is played on an 8x8 board. The game starts with two discs of each color placed diagonally in the four center squares. Black always moves first.
Flanking
A move places a disc on an empty square so that it flanks - traps between your new disc and another disc of yours - one or more opponent discs in a straight line (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally). Every flanked disc flips to your color immediately.

When you can't move

No legal move
If you have no legal move, your turn is skipped automatically and play passes back to your opponent - there's no way to voluntarily pass while a legal move exists. If neither player has a legal move, the game ends immediately.

How a game ends

Winning
The game ends the moment neither player has a legal move (usually because the board is full). Whoever has more discs on the board wins; an equal split is a draw.
Resignation
A player can resign at any time, ending the game immediately in the opponent's favor.
