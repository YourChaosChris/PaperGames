Spider Solitaire Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays Spider Solitaire - useful if you're rusty, or learning.

The basic idea

K♠ The layout
Two full decks (104 cards) are dealt into 10 tableau columns - the first 4 columns get 6 cards each, the other 6 get 5 cards each - with only the top card of every column face-up. There are no foundations. The remaining 50 cards sit in the stock, dealt out later in batches of 10.
Choosing a difficulty
Before starting, pick how many suits are in play: 1 suit is easiest (every card of the right rank can extend any run), 2 suits is medium, and 4 suits is the traditional, hardest version - exactly like two real decks shuffled together.
Moving a single card
A card can move onto a tableau column only onto a card exactly one rank higher - suit doesn't matter for this. An empty column accepts any card, or any valid run, with no rank restriction at all.
Moving a group of cards
Several cards already stacked together can only be moved as one unit if they're already sitting in a properly descending run of the SAME suit (for example a 5-4-3 of Hearts, in that exact order) - moving a run that mixes suits, even if the ranks descend correctly, isn't allowed. Moving a group still follows the same-rank-higher placement rule above. Once cards move away, the card newly exposed on top of its column automatically turns face-up.
The stock
Tap the stock to deal its next batch of 10 cards, one face-up card onto each of the 10 columns. Dealing is blocked while any column is empty - clear it first (by moving its cards elsewhere or completing a run through it) before the stock can be dealt again.
Completing a sequence
Whenever a complete run from King down to Ace, all the same suit and in order, ends up contiguous in a column, it's detected automatically and removed - no need for it to be the only cards in that column, just properly stacked at the point where they'd naturally lift off together. Each completed run adds one to the counter above the board.

Finishing a game

Winning
The game is won once all 8 King-to-Ace runs have been assembled and removed - that's 104 cards / 13, the same total regardless of which suit-count difficulty you picked.
