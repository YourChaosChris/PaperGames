Tested devices

PaperGames runs in the web browser of an e-reader. Which readers it really works on is only known where someone has tried it and reported back. This page lists those devices, and it says just as clearly what has not been tested.

Check it on your own device

1. Open the page
Open PaperGames in your reader's own browser. Look out for: the page loads, the text is sharp and nothing is cut off at the side.
2. Start a game
Start a game, for example Sudoku or Chess, and make a few moves. Look out for: every tap lands on the square you meant, and the board fits on the screen without scrolling sideways.
3. Open the settings
Open Settings and switch the text size and high contrast. Look out for: the page changes at once and everything stays readable.
4. Go offline
Wait until the home page says that PaperGames is ready offline, then switch off Wi-Fi and reload the page. Look out for: the page and a game still open.
5. Come back later
Leave a game in the middle, close the browser and open it again later. Look out for: the game is exactly where you left it.

Report back

Send the name of your device and, if you can, a screenshot of what you saw to this address:
