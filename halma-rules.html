Halma Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays Halma - useful if you're rusty, or learning.

The basic idea

The setup
Each player starts with ten pieces filling a triangular camp in one corner of the board, and must be the first to move every one of them into the camp in the diagonally opposite corner. Player 1 moves first.
Moving
On your turn, either move one piece a single square in any of the 8 directions into an empty square, or make one or more chained jumps: hop over an adjacent piece (yours or the opponent's - nothing is captured) onto the empty square directly beyond it, then optionally keep jumping from there. A turn is either exactly one step, or a jump sequence of any length - you can stop after any jump rather than being forced to continue.
Winning
The first player to get all ten of their pieces into their goal camp wins - it doesn't matter if a few of the opponent's pieces are still there too, only your own count.

A note on strategy

Chains are the key
A single long jump chain can move a piece most of the way across the board in one turn, so building a corridor of pieces your own piece can hop down is usually far faster than walking step by step.
