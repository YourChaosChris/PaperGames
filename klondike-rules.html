Klondike Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays Klondike - useful if you're rusty, or learning.

The basic idea

K♦ The layout
Cards are dealt into 7 tableau columns of 1 through 7 cards each, with only the top card of every column face-up. Four foundations (one per suit) are built up from Ace to King. The rest of the deck sits in a stock pile, drawn one card at a time onto a waste pile.
Moving cards
A card can move onto a tableau column only onto a card exactly one rank higher and the opposite color, or onto any empty column, which accepts a King only. A run of several cards already stacked face-up in that same descending, alternating order can be moved together as one unit onto a valid destination.
Foundations
A foundation only accepts its own suit, starting with the Ace and moving up one rank at a time. Once a card is on a foundation it stays there - foundations only receive cards, they never give them back to the tableau.
The stock and waste
Tap the stock pile to flip its top card face-up onto the waste pile, one card at a time. Once the stock is empty, tapping it again reshuffles the whole waste pile back into the stock so you can draw through it again - PaperGames doesn't limit how many times you can redeal.

Finishing a game

Winning
The game is won the moment all four foundations are complete, Ace through King. Not every shuffle can be won - unlike FreeCell, a Klondike deal can genuinely become unsolvable partway through, since only the top card of each tableau column is visible up front.
