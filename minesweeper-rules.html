Minesweeper Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays Minesweeper - useful if you're rusty, or learning.

The basic idea

The grid and the first click
Hidden mines are scattered across the grid. Your first reveal is always guaranteed safe - the mines are only placed afterward, avoiding that cell and everything around it - so the game never ends before it begins.
Reading the numbers
Revealing a safe cell shows a number: how many mines are hiding in its eight neighboring cells. A blank cell means zero, and revealing it automatically opens up every connected blank area for you.
Flagging
Mark a cell you believe hides a mine with a flag, using Flag mode (for touchscreens) or a right-click (on a desktop browser) - flagged cells can't be accidentally revealed. Clicking an already-revealed number whose flagged neighbors match its count reveals the rest of its neighbors at once, exactly like a middle-click in the classic desktop game - but a wrongly placed flag can still cost you the game this way.

How a game ends

Winning
Reveal every cell that isn't a mine and you've cleared the board.
Losing
Reveal a mine and the game ends immediately - every mine on the board is shown, with the one that got you marked apart from the rest.
