Amazons Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays The Game of the Amazons - useful if you're rusty, or learning.

The basic idea

The setup
Each player has four amazons on a 10x10 board, placed in the standard symmetric starting position. There is no capturing anywhere in this game - it is won purely by movement and territory.
Moving an amazon
On your turn, move one of your own amazons any number of empty squares in a straight line - horizontally, vertically, or diagonally - exactly like a chess queen. It can never jump over another amazon or a burned square.
Shooting an arrow
Immediately after moving, that same amazon must shoot an arrow from its new square: another queen-style slide, in any of the 8 directions, through empty squares only, landing on an empty square.
Burned squares
Wherever an arrow lands, that square is burned for the rest of the game - permanently impassable terrain that no amazon and no future arrow can ever cross or land on again.

Winning the game

How the game ends
If it's your turn and none of your amazons has a single legal move left - blocked in every direction by other amazons, burned squares, or the edge of the board - you lose. The last player still able to move wins.
