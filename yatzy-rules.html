Yatzy Rules

A quick reference for how PaperGames plays Yatzy - useful if you're rusty, or learning.

The basic idea

The dice
Each turn you roll five six-sided dice. After the roll, tap any die to hold it - held dice keep their value - then roll again to reroll the rest. You get up to three rolls per turn in total.
The scoresheet
After your final roll of the turn, tap one of the 15 categories in the scoresheet to score your dice there. Each category can only be used once per game, so a bad roll still has to go somewhere - the game ends once all 15 are filled in.
Upper section: Ones to Sixes
These six categories score the sum of the matching dice - for example, three fours scores 12 in the Fours row. Fill in all six and reach a combined 63 points or more, and you get a 50-point bonus on top.
Lower section: combinations
One Pair and Two Pairs score the sum of the matching dice, taking the highest pair(s) available - a four-of-a-kind roll still counts as two pairs of the same number. Three of a Kind and Four of a Kind score the sum of just the three or four matching dice, not all five. Small Straight (1-2-3-4-5) and Large Straight (2-3-4-5-6) score a fixed 15 and 20. Full House (three of one number plus a pair of another) scores the sum of all five dice. Chance scores the sum of all five dice, whatever they show. Yatzy - all five dice the same - scores a fixed 50.

Finishing a game

Final score
Once every category is filled in, your final score is the upper section total (plus its 50-point bonus, if earned) added to the lower section total. There's no opponent to beat - just your own best score to chase next time.
