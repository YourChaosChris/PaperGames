The History of Klappenkasten

A compact pub game

Shut-the-box is a traditional dice game associated with British pubs and sailors. A hinged wooden box holds numbered tiles; players roll dice and fold down combinations that match the roll. The compact kit made it easy to carry and play on a table.

Many local versions

The number of flaps and the scoring vary across sets and regions. Some boxes run from 1 to 9, others extend to 12, and some allow a single die after the higher flaps have been closed. This PaperGames version uses 1 to 9 and the one-die rule after 7, 8 and 9 are shut.
